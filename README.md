# The Map of Me

A single-file personal trait map for Grade 9 STEAM, The Blueprint of Life, Lesson 1: Heredity and the Map of Me. It replaces a Jamboard activity that no longer works.

Students build the map the way the activity lays it out:

- A grid of inherited traits (the same grid as the class activity, with "The Map of Me" in the centre). Students star each trait that describes them, and add a unique trait of their own.
- Question 1: name 5 things about themselves that they did NOT inherit and are not traits.
- Question 2: pick which of six statements are NOT traits.
- Question 3: choose one and explain why it is not a trait.
- Question 4: explain how talents can have a genetic component and also depend on nurture or environment.
- Question 5: define trait and give 3 examples.

When they are done, the app compiles everything into one printable map page that they can print or copy into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
