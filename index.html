Grade 9 STEAM, The Blueprint of Life, Lesson 1: Heredity and the Map of Me

Heredity: what features have you inherited? Build your map, then answer the questions that follow. Take your time: this map is about you. Your answers stay on this page and nothing is sent anywhere. When you finish, print or copy your map to keep it with your work.

My inherited traits

Select each trait that describes you to give it a star. Select it again to take the star away.

Question 1: Not inherited

Name 5 things about yourself that you did NOT inherit and are not traits. For example: I have a sister, and I have 3 dogs. These are facts about me that are not inherited traits.

Question 2: Which are NOT traits?

Which of the following are NOT traits? Select each one that is not a trait.

Question 3: Explain your thinking

Choose one of the answers you selected in Question 2 and explain why it is not considered to be a trait.

Question 4: Nature and nurture

Talents like athletic ability or musical ability have a genetic component but also depend on nurture or your environment. Explain this statement.

Question 5: What is a trait?

Define trait, then give 3 examples of traits.
